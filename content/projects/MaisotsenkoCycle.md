A hybrid air conditioning system that uses the Maisotsenko Cycle to pre-cool the air entering a heat pump condenser, improving efficiency and reducing energy use. Developed as my Bachelor Thesis, this project demonstrates how innovative cooling strategies can make buildings more sustainable. Key results include up to 15% energy savings and a significant boost in system performance. For more details, see the full thesis linked below.
