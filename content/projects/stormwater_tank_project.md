Design and development of a stormwater retention tank aimed at preventing urban flooding during heavy rainfall events. The project included hydraulic calculations, structural considerations, and the definition of all construction elements such as piping, chambers, inlet systems, and safety criteria. The final document delivered a complete, build-ready design aligned with environmental and regulatory requirements.
