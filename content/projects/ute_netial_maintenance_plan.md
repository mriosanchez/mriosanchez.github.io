As part of my role at UTE Netial, I updated the company’s General Maintenance Plan by integrating all newly acquired machinery into a unified and structured preventive maintenance system. This included reviewing manufacturer guidelines, defining maintenance levels (A/B/C), and creating detailed maintenance sheets for each vehicle and equipment type. The result was a complete, standardized and scalable plan that improved traceability, compliance and workshop efficiency.
